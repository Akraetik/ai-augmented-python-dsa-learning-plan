# 02-3 Two Pointers Rapid Fire Drill

**Narrator:** Section two, track three. Rapid fire. Say it before you hear it. Repeat this track until it's easy.

*[pause 2s]*

**Narrator:** Two pointers turns which Big O into which?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** O of n squared into O of n.

**Narrator:** Sorted list, find a pair. Shape?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Opposite ends.

**Narrator:** Reads the same both ways. Shape?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Opposite ends.

**Narrator:** In order across two strings. Shape?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Same direction. One pointer each.

**Narrator:** Maximize something capped by the shorter side. Shape?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Shrink the weaker side.

**Narrator:** Three numbers that sum to zero. Shape?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Sort, fix one, two pointers on the rest.

**Narrator:** Sum too small. Which pointer?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Left moves right.

**Narrator:** Sum too big. Which pointer?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Right moves left.

**Narrator:** Why drop the left number when the sum is too small?

*[pause 4s]*

**Answer:** Even the biggest partner isn't enough.

**Narrator:** Two Sum two returns what kind of index?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** One-indexed.

**Narrator:** Palindrome skip loops need what guard?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Left less than right.

**Narrator:** Squares of a sorted array. Biggest squares are where?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** At the ends.

**Narrator:** And you fill the answer from?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** The back.

**Narrator:** Is Subsequence. Fast pointer is in which string?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** t, the longer one.

**Narrator:** Slow pointer moves when?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** On a match.

**Narrator:** Container area?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Width times the shorter line.

**Narrator:** Container. Which line moves?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** The shorter one.

**Narrator:** Container. Why?

*[pause 4s]*

**Answer:** Narrower and no taller can't win.

**Narrator:** Three Sum. First step?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Sort.

**Narrator:** Three Sum Big O?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** n squared.

**Narrator:** Three Sum. Skip a fixed value when it equals which neighbor?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** The one before it.

**Narrator:** Three Sum. After a match, what then?

*[pause 4s]*

**Answer:** Move both pointers past repeats.

**Narrator:** Three Sum. Fixed number is positive?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Break.

**Narrator:** Three Sum Closest. Need to skip duplicates?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** No.

**Narrator:** Two pointer water. Which side do you process?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** The side with the smaller max.

**Narrator:** Two pointer water. Space?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** O of one.

**Narrator:** Less than, or less than or equal, for pairs?

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Less than. A pair needs two different items.

**Narrator:** New bug. Too small, but you move right. Name it.

*[pause 3s]*

**Answer:** Moving the wrong pointer.

**Narrator:** Unsorted list, find a pair that sums to target. Two pointers?

*[pause 4s]*

**Answer:** No. Use a dictionary. Or sort first, if positions don't matter.

*[pause 1s]*

**Narrator:** End of drill.
